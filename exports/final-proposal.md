Custom Reporting & Marketing Page Setup Proposal for SaaSyKit
Executive Summary
I propose to deliver a custom reporting solution as a work-for-hire developer on your SaaSyKit
platform. This will include core reporting features with filtering by rep, advertiser, product, and
fields, plus print and export capabilities. Additionally, I will set up editable public static marketing
pages for your conversion funnel, ensuring they require no developer involvement for basic
content management.

Scope & Deliverables
●​ Develop a custom reporting plugin/component for SaaSyKit with:
○​ Filtering and segmentation by rep, advertiser, product, and fields (aligned with legacy
app functionality)
○​ Print and export (PDF/Excel) capabilities
●​ Coordinate and support your lead developer regarding the integration of the custom
reporting code.
●​ Set up public static marketing pages for the conversion funnel, including:
○​ Theme selection and installation.
○​ Configuration for basic content management, allowing non-developers to update
content.
●​ Provide basic documentation and handoff notes for both the reporting and marketing
pages.
●​ Iterative feedback cycles and adjustments included.
Out of Scope
The following items are explicitly outside the scope of this initial proposal:
●​ Integration with third-party advertising platforms (e.g., AdvertServe).
●​ Integration with CRM or marketing automation platforms (e.g., HubSpot).
●​ Development of interactive charts or advanced data visualizations beyond basic tabular
reports with print/export.
●​ Any modifications to SaaSyKit's core multi-tenancy or user management systems.
●​ Ongoing maintenance or long-term support beyond the initial engagement, unless
separately agreed upon.
